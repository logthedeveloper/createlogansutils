# Create: Logans Utils
**Create: Logans Utils** adds a munch of things I whish were in base create along with other things.

### Full Content List
- **Blaze Milk Cake** has the effect of milk. Spout milk into blaze cake base.
- **Sulfur Dust** used to craft gunpowder. Crushed dirt gives you this powder. spout lava to make gunpowder
- **Cheese Bowl** used to make cheesy break. spout milk onto bowl
- **Cheesy Bread Bread** made by mixing cheese bowl/cheese slice while heated with bread. Gives max saturation and fills hunger
- **Cheese Slice Made** with a block of cheese. Crushing gives 9 with a chance for another, Crafting gives 9.
- **FEESH Fish Weapon.** Gives crazy knockback with no damage.
- **Young & Aged Cheese Bin** Spouting milk into a composter turns it into a young cheese bin. Spout again to age it or place it on the ground and wait ~10 seconds. Right clicking aged cheese bin drops the cheese block out of it.
- **Cheese Block** Used to make cheese slices, can be turned into liquid cheese
- **Liquid Cheese** used to make cheese recipes. More soon.
- **Mint Can** be found across the world. Used to make Mint Tea
- **Empty Cup** Crafted with glass. Used to make tea
- **Mint Tea** Spout liquid mint into empty cup. Regeneration 5 for 15 seconds and Jump boost 3 for 5.
- **Mint Seeds** Used to plant mint. Harvesting gives mint
- **Liquid Redstone** New fuel type. Used in blaze burners to super heat, pools of it can be found across the world and underground (Like Lava!) and occasionally redstone blocks will spawn around the pool. Putting a bucket of liquid redstone into a crafting table gives you a redstone block. Liquid redstone crafted by mixing redstone block and cinder flour over a heated basin.
### Non Mod item Recipes
- **Sapling Crushing** Crushing saplings with 2 crushing wheels gives the leaves of the sapling and apples if they are normally dropped.
- **Honey Comb** Spout honey onto iron sheet to make honeycomb.
- **Heavy Core** 4 Lodestones 4 Diamond blocks and a Netherite Block. Perfect for crafting a Mace in Peaceful Worlds.
- **Liquid Honey** Mix Sugar over a heated Basin. Makes honey
- **Sponge Washing** a sponge makes that sponge wet and a 0.5 chance for another sponge to spawn in. Makes sponges renewable.
